What's new in Keel

Updated 2026-11-12

Every version of Keel, what it added and what it fixed. The same notes appear in the App Store.

1.1.0 · 10 November 2026

Keel can now read your subscriptions for you.

New

  • Keel reads it: hand Keel screenshots, PDF statements or a rough typed list, and it comes back as entries to review. Read by Apple's private cloud model. Opt-in, needs iOS 27 and an Apple Intelligence iPhone.
  • Everything is previewed before it is sent to the AI. Card numbers, IBANs, phone numbers and references are removed automatically on the phone, and you can cut anything else.
  • Lists grouped by account land in the right account and currency. Prices, payment mode, billing cycle and start dates are read off your own words.
  • The review screen is grouped by account. Tap a chip to place an item on an account, or create one on the spot. It says which lines could not be read.
  • Imported items arrive with earlier months paid. This month stays open when payment is set manual.
  • Your own AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) is still there, one swipe away, with the same instructions.
  • Upcoming shows "Changes ahead": every trial ending and every price change in the next three months, with the date and the new price.

Improved

  • Plainer wording across the app, in all five languages.
  • Insights and reminders use the price in force today, not the trial price a subscription started with.
  • Turning iCloud backup off can delete the copy in iCloud too.
  • With the app lock on, reminders on the lock screen no longer show the name or the amount.

Fixed

  • Subscriptions billed on the 29th, 30th or 31st no longer drift to the 28th after February.
  • Editing a paused-then-resumed subscription no longer logs the paused months as paid.
  • A manual subscription with an end date keeps its last charge until you tick it.
  • Moving a subscription's first date into the past records the payments in between, and an automatic charge never shows as overdue after an edit.
  • "Today" is your local day everywhere.
  • A purchase that completes after the App Store sheet closes unlocks Full Keel at the next launch.
  • Restoring a backup or importing a file backs up what it replaces first.
  • Widgets no longer show sample rows on a real home screen, and follow a theme change at once.

Keel now requires iOS 16.4 or later.
