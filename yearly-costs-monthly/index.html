Yearly costs, smoothed into a monthly number

Updated 2026-11-08

A yearly bill costs you every month, you just pay it once. Keel shows both views: the month as it will actually hit your accounts, and your real monthly baseline with every yearly cost spread across twelve months.

Why the monthly total lies

Add up your monthly subscriptions and you get a comfortable number. Then the car insurance, the licence fee, the domain renewals and the annual software plan all land between November and January, and the comfortable number was never true. Budget apps call the fix a sinking fund: divide each yearly cost by twelve and set that aside every month. Doing it by hand means a spreadsheet that nobody updates after March.

What Keel does with a yearly item

ViewWhat it shows
Upcoming and the calendarThe real charge on its real date, with the account it leaves. This is what your bank balance will see.
Monthly baseline in InsightsEach yearly, half-yearly or quarterly cost divided over its period and added to the monthly charges. This is what the year costs you per month.
Cadence breakdownHow much of your baseline is monthly, how much is yearly ambushes, by category and by account.
Paid versus plannedWhat you really paid so far next to what the rest of the year still plans to charge.

Multi-currency, the same way

A yearly fee in euros and a monthly plan in francs land in the same baseline, converted at daily rates. Keel shows the original currency on the item and your display currency in the totals.

Price changes count from their date

When a yearly plan announces a new price, Keel keeps the old price until the change date and uses the new one after it. Upcoming shows "Changes ahead": every trial ending and every price change in the next three months, with the date and the new price. Insights use the price in force today, not the price you signed up at.

Reminders before the ambush

Each yearly item can carry up to three reminders, with any lead time: a month before to move money, a week before to decide whether to renew, the day before to check the account. On the lock screen, with the app lock on, the reminder shows neither the name nor the amount.

What you need

Keel runs on iPhone, iOS 16.4 or later, with no account and no bank connection. It is free for 45 days, then one purchase of 12 CHF during the trial or 15 CHF after. The monthly baseline, the charts and the history stay visible even if you never buy.
