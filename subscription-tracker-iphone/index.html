A subscription tracker for iPhone that is not itself a subscription

Updated 2026-11-08

Keel is a subscription tracker for iPhone with no account, no server and no bank connection. It is free for 45 days, then one purchase, and it tracks subscriptions, bills and installment purchases per bank account.

Download on theApp Store

What "no account, no server" means in practice

  • There is nothing to sign up for. Keel opens and works.
  • Your list lives in the app's storage on your iPhone, behind Face ID if you turn the lock on.
  • Backups are files you create, or a copy in your own iCloud if you enable it. Keel has no copy anywhere.
  • The app talks to four services and nothing else: exchange rates, logos if you turn them on, Apple's cloud model if you turn it on, and the App Store for the purchase. The privacy policy lists them.

What Keel tracks

Setting up without an afternoon of typing

Most trackers die on day one because entering everything takes an afternoon. Keel offers three ways in, and you choose.

  • Known services come pre-filled: tap Netflix, Spotify or your phone carrier and adjust the price.
  • Keel reads it: hand it screenshots, PDF statements or a rough typed list, and it comes back as entries to review. This runs on Apple's Private Cloud Compute, is off until you turn it on, and removes card numbers, IBANs and references on the phone before anything is sent.
  • Your own AI: paste the same list into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini with Keel's instructions, paste the answer back.

Every imported line stops at a review screen before it is saved. If you want no AI at all, one switch in Settings removes every AI surface from the app.

What you get that a list cannot give

  • Upcoming: what leaves which account, day by day, with overdue items and what is already paid.
  • A calendar with each service on its due day.
  • Insights: what you really paid next to what the year still plans to charge, spending by category and by account, and price creep since the day you signed up. How yearly costs become a monthly number.
  • Reminders your way, up to three per item, and widgets for the home and lock screen.
  • Five languages: English, French, German, Italian, Polish. Multi-currency with daily rates.

Price

Everything is free for 45 days from the first commitment you add. Then one purchase unlocks Full Keel: 12 CHF during the trial, 15 CHF after, shared with up to six people through Family Sharing. If you never buy, your list, charts, history, marking paid and export keep working.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later. Reading with Apple's cloud model needs iOS 27 and an Apple Intelligence iPhone. There is no Android or web version yet.
