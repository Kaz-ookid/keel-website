Support

Keel is made and supported by one person. Write to contactkeel@protonmail.com and you get an answer from the person who wrote the app, usually within two days.

What helps in a message

  • Your iOS version and iPhone model (Settings > General > About).
  • The Keel version (Settings > About, inside the app).
  • What you did, what you expected, what happened instead. A screenshot helps. Never send bank details.

Common questions

  • Purchase not recognised: open Full Keel in the app and tap Restore. A purchase that completes after the App Store sheet closes is picked up at the next launch.
  • Reminders not showing: check that notifications are allowed for Keel in iOS Settings, and that the item has a reminder set.
  • Moving to a new iPhone: export a backup from Settings, or turn on iCloud backup in Keel before the move, then restore on the new phone.
  • Privacy: Keel has no account and no server. The privacy policy names every network request the app makes.
