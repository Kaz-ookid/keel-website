How to track pay-in-4 and installment purchases on iPhone

Updated 2026-11-08

Keel tracks an installment purchase as one item with its provider, its bank account, the amount still owed and its end date. Each installment sits on the calendar with your subscriptions, so the month shows what really leaves.

The problem with installment plans

A pay-in-4 purchase is not a subscription. It has a fixed number of charges, a known end, and a provider (Klarna, PayPal, Afterpay, Affirm, a store's own plan) that is not the shop you bought from. Subscription trackers can only fake it as a monthly subscription, which hides the end date and the amount left. Banking apps show the charge after it happened. Neither tells you that three plans end in March and two new ones start in April.

Keel was built around this gap. The installment plan is its own kind of item, next to subscriptions and bills, and the same calendar carries all three.

What Keel records for each plan

FieldWhy it matters
Total and number of installmentsKeel derives each charge, or you enter the per-installment amount if the plan states it that way.
ProviderThe name that shows on your statement, so a Klarna charge is recognisable as the sofa, not as "Klarna".
Bank account or cardEvery installment knows which account it leaves. Filter by account to see what one card owes this month.
First charge date and cadenceWeekly, every two weeks, monthly. Keel places every remaining installment on the calendar.
Paid so far, amount left, end dateThe progress you actually want to know, visible on the item and in Insights.

Adding a plan takes a minute

  • By hand: choose "pay in installments", type the total, the count and the first date. Keel fills the rest.
  • From the confirmation email or a screenshot: Keel reads it, with Apple's Private Cloud Compute if you turn that on, or through your own AI with Keel's instructions, and you review each line before it is saved.
  • From a typed list: "airpods pay-in-4 179 via paypal from 20 May" is enough. Keel reads the provider, the count, the amount and the start.

What you see afterwards

  • Upcoming: the next installment of each plan, among your subscriptions and bills, grouped by day, with the account it leaves.
  • Calendar: every installment on its date, with the provider's logo if you turn logos on.
  • Insights: how much of the month is installments versus subscriptions, and when the plans end.
  • Reminders: up to three per plan, any lead time, so a charge on a card you rarely use is never a surprise.
  • Widgets: the next payments on your home screen or lock screen.

Privacy

Keel never connects to your bank or to the installment provider. You enter the plan, or you let Keel read a document you chose. The data stays on your iPhone, with no account and no server. The privacy policy names every network request the app makes.
